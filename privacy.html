Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

OddsAway ("we", "us", "our") operates this website. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you visit OddsAway.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Email address — if you sign up to our newsletter or download a guide
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, device type (via analytics tools)
  • Cookies — small files stored on your device to improve your experience and track affiliate referrals

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To send you travel guides, destination updates, and editorial newsletters (only if you subscribed)
  • To understand how visitors use our site and improve our content
  • To track affiliate referrals as required by our commercial partners

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Affiliate Disclosure

OddsAway participates in affiliate marketing programmes. When you click a link to a casino, sportsbook, or travel partner and make a qualifying action (e.g. a deposit), we may receive a commission. This never affects the independence or accuracy of our editorial content.

We only recommend products and services we have researched and believe are suitable for our readers.

4. Cookies

We use cookies for:

  • Analytics (understanding site traffic and usage patterns)
  • Affiliate tracking (attributing referrals to our partners)
  • Preferences (remembering your settings)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though this may affect site functionality.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Analytics — website usage tracking
  • Mailchimp / ConvertKit — email newsletter delivery
  • Netlify — website hosting

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to request deletion of your data
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time (e.g. unsubscribe from emails)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at: privacy@oddsaway.co

7. Data Retention

We retain email subscriber data for as long as you remain subscribed. Analytics data is retained per the default settings of each analytics provider. You may request deletion at any time.

8. Children

OddsAway is strictly for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has submitted data to us, please contact us immediately.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions: privacy@oddsaway.co
