Best European Cities for a Casino Holiday

🗓 Updated February 2026 ⏱ ~8 min read ✈️ Destination Guide

Whether you're after the glamour of Monte Carlo, the sun-soaked tables of Malta, or a city break with a spin thrown in — Europe has more world-class gambling destinations than most people realise.

This guide covers the best European cities for a casino holiday: where to go, what to play, and what to know before you fly.

1. 🇲🇨 Monte Carlo, Monaco — The Iconic Choice

Best for: High rollers, first-timers who want the full experience, Formula 1 fans

There's a reason Monte Carlo is the first thing people picture when they think "European casino holiday." The Casino de Monte-Carlo has been operating since 1863, and the building itself is worth the trip — baroque architecture, chandelier-lit gaming floors, the works.

What to Know

  • Minimum bets are higher than most European casinos — budget accordingly
  • The Grand Prix circuit wraps around the casino district every May — book a trip in early May for the full spectacle
  • Monaco residents are actually banned from gambling inside — the casino exists for visitors
  • Day trips from Nice (30 min by train) are very doable if you're on a budget

Best casinos: Casino de Monte-Carlo, Casino Café de Paris, Sun Casino

Betting legality: Sports betting available at licensed venues. Online betting widely accessible.

2. 🇲🇹 Malta — Europe's Hidden Gambling Capital

Best for: Sun + casino combo, longer stays, online gambling industry types

Malta punches well above its weight. For a tiny island, it has an outsized presence in the global gambling industry — the Malta Gaming Authority (MGA) licenses hundreds of operators worldwide, and the island hosts the iGaming Capital of Europe.

For the travelling bettor, that means well-run casinos, a strong sports betting culture, and a cluster of high-end venues in St Julian's and Paceville.

What to Know

  • Climate is excellent year-round — the casino holiday that doesn't require sun cream luck
  • Dragonara Casino in St Julian's is the flagship, set inside a 19th-century palazzo on a private peninsula
  • Malta's online betting scene is excellent — most major operators are MGA-licensed, so you're in safe hands
  • English is an official language — zero communication issues

Best casinos: Dragonara Casino, Oracle Casino, Portomaso Casino

Betting legality: Fully regulated. Sports betting legal and widespread.

3. 🇫🇷 Paris & Deauville, France — Old World Elegance

Best for: Culture + casino combo, horse racing fans

France has a long and complicated relationship with gambling — casinos are technically banned in Paris proper, but Deauville (2 hours by train) more than makes up for it. Deauville is Monaco's quieter, more French cousin: a seaside resort town with a grand casino, excellent horse racing, and a food scene that will wreck your diet.

What to Know

  • Casino Barrière de Deauville is one of the most beautiful casino buildings in Europe
  • Deauville hosts major horse racing including the Prix Morny — perfect for the racing bettor
  • Paris itself has off-track betting (PMU) outlets everywhere for horses and sports
  • August in Deauville is peak season — book well ahead

Best casinos: Casino Barrière de Deauville, Casino Barrière d'Étretat

Betting legality: Sports betting regulated via licensed operators (FDJ, Unibet FR). Casinos restricted to resort towns.

4. 🇬🇧 London, UK — Easiest for British Bettors

Best for: Poker players, Cheltenham / Royal Ascot fans, those who want betting shops on every corner

The UK has the most mature and regulated gambling market in Europe. London alone has dozens of licensed casinos, from the Grosvenor Victoria to the Hippodrome in Leicester Square. And if you're travelling for a major racing event — Cheltenham Festival in March, Royal Ascot in June — London is your base.

What to Know

  • Membership requirements exist at some casinos, though many are open-access
  • Betting shops (William Hill, Ladbrokes, Coral, Betfred) are everywhere — more accessible than any other European city
  • Hippodrome Casino near Trafalgar Square is a great all-rounder: slots, poker, shows, bars
  • Cheltenham Festival week is four days of world-class racing, excellent atmosphere, and serious betting action

Best casinos: The Hippodrome, Grosvenor Victoria, Aspers at Westfield

Betting legality: Fully regulated by the UK Gambling Commission. Best market for transparency and consumer protection.

5. 🇦🇹 Vienna, Austria — The Underrated Pick

Best for: City break + casino, culture + play combo, poker enthusiasts

Vienna is criminally underrated on the casino holiday circuit. The Casinos Austria group runs high-quality venues across the country, and the flagship Vienna casino — inside the Palais Esterhazy — is exceptional. Add in world-class restaurants, opera, and coffee culture, and Vienna is a genuine alternative to the obvious choices.

What to Know

  • Poker is taken seriously in Vienna — good tournament scene
  • The casino is connected to a luxury hotel, making it a genuine resort experience
  • Austria is centrally located — easy to combine with other European stops
  • Less crowded and more affordable than Monaco or London

Best casinos: Casino Wien, Casino Baden (30 min from Vienna)

Betting legality: Sports betting regulated by Austrian law. Online betting accessible through licensed operators.

6. 🇪🇸 Barcelona, Spain — Sports Betting Hub

Best for: Football fans, sports bettors, beach + bet combo

Barcelona is one of the great European cities, and its gambling scene has developed significantly in recent years. The Gran Casino Barcelona — located right on the waterfront — is one of the best-positioned casinos on the continent. Combine it with Camp Nou, La Boqueria, and the beach, and you have a near-perfect sports holiday.

What to Know

  • Spanish sports betting (regulated by DGOJ) is competitive and well-developed
  • Gran Casino Barcelona sits in the Port Olímpic marina — spectacular setting
  • If you're planning around a Barça or Atlético match, book accommodation well ahead
  • Online sports betting apps are widely available in Spain

Best casinos: Gran Casino Barcelona, Casino Barcelona

Betting legality: Sports betting fully regulated. Online gambling legal and well-developed.

Before You Go: 5 Things Every Casino Traveller Should Know

  1. Check the legal age. It's 18 across most of Europe, but verify for each country.
  2. Bring ID. Virtually every European casino will ask for identification on entry.
  3. Know the dress code. Monte Carlo has one. Malta and London, generally less strict — but smart casual is always safe.
  4. Set a budget before you land. Decide your total holiday gambling budget before you're in the room. It's much easier to stick to it that way.
  5. Use licensed online operators. If you're betting from your hotel, stick to MGA, UKGC, or local-authority licensed sites. Avoid unlicensed operators.

🌍 The Bottom Line: Europe's casino holiday scene is more varied — and more accessible — than most people realise. You don't need to fly to Vegas or Macau for a world-class gambling experience. From Monte Carlo's old-world glamour to Malta's sunny efficiency, the continent has an option for every budget, every taste, and every type of bettor.
