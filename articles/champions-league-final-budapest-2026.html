Why Budapest Is Worth the Trip

Budapest is genuinely one of Europe's great cities — and it's significantly underpriced compared to Paris, London, or Milan. Think thermal baths, ruin bars, stunning architecture, and some of the best value food and drink in the EU.

For the sports bettor, it adds something extra: Hungary's casino scene is small but accessible, and the atmosphere around a major sporting event is electric.

Getting There

The Weekend Itinerary

Thursday

Arrive & Explore

  • Check in and head to the ruin bars in District VII — Szimpla Kert is the classic, opens from the afternoon
  • Walk the Jewish Quarter — excellent food and drink, great energy
  • Place your pre-match bets at Tippmix or via your preferred online platform
Friday

Build the Atmosphere

  • Morning: Széchenyi Thermal Baths — a Budapest essential, massive outdoor pools
  • Afternoon: Chain Bridge, Buda Castle, and Parliament from the Danube
  • Evening: Andrássy Avenue for dinner and the Las Vegas Casino for table action
Saturday

Match Day 🏆

  • Morning: Sports bars opening early — look for Fradi Fan Club area for atmosphere
  • Afternoon: Head toward Puskás Arena early — fan zone opens 4–5 hours before kick-off
  • Match: 21:00 CET kick-off (Budapest is UTC+2 in summer)
  • Night: Post-match — District VII ruin bars will be packed and electric
Sunday

Recovery & Departure

  • Central Market Hall for late breakfast and a souvenir run
  • Gerbeaud Café on Vörösmarty Square for coffee and cake
  • Afternoon flights usually doable for a Sunday departure

Where to Stay

The Puskás Arena is in Pest, easily reached from any central hotel. Aim for District V (inner city), District VI, or District VII for the best combination of access and atmosphere.
