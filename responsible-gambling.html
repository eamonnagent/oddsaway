Responsible Gambling

OddsAway is committed to promoting safe and responsible gambling.

🛑 If gambling is causing you harm, please stop and seek help. Free, confidential support is available 24/7 — see the resources below.

Our Commitment

OddsAway is a travel and destination guide for adults who enjoy gambling as part of their leisure activities. We believe gambling should be fun, social, and within your means — never a source of stress, financial harm, or addiction.

All gambling content on OddsAway is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We encourage all readers to gamble responsibly and within their means.

Are You Gambling Safely?

Gambling is safe and enjoyable when it stays within these boundaries:

  • You set a budget before you play — and stick to it
  • You gamble for entertainment, not to make money
  • You never chase losses
  • It doesn't affect your relationships, work, or finances
  • You can stop whenever you choose to

Warning Signs to Watch For

Seek help if gambling is:

  • Taking up more time or money than you intended
  • Causing arguments with family or friends
  • Making you feel anxious, guilty, or depressed
  • Leading you to borrow money or sell possessions
  • Something you hide from others
  • Something you feel unable to stop

Self-Exclusion Tools

All reputable casinos and online sportsbooks offer self-exclusion and deposit limit tools. Before travelling to a casino destination, you can:

  • Set a loss limit before you travel — a hard number you won't go beyond
  • Use self-exclusion on any online accounts if online gambling is an issue
  • Tell a trusted person your budget for the trip
  • Use pre-paid cards with a fixed amount rather than direct bank access
