## How to Structure the Weekend

### Thursday — Arrive & Explore
- Check into your hotel and head to the **ruin bars** in District VII (Szimpla Kert is the classic, opens from the afternoon)
- Walk the **Jewish Quarter** — excellent food and drink, good energy
- Place your pre-match bets at Tippmix or via your preferred online platform

### Friday — Build the Atmosphere
- Morning: **Széchenyi Thermal Baths** — a Budapest essential, massive outdoor pools, works out any travel stiffness
- Afternoon: Walk the **Chain Bridge**, Buda Castle, and Parliament from the Danube
- Evening: **Andrássy Avenue** for dinner and the Las Vegas Casino for some table action

### Saturday — Match Day
- Morning: Sports bars opening early (look for **Fradi Fan Club** area bars for atmosphere)
- Afternoon: Head toward Puskás Arena early — the fan zone opens 4–5 hours before kick-off
- Match: 21:00 CET kick-off (check local time — Budapest is UTC+2 in summer)
- Night: Post-match celebrations — District VII ruin bars will be packed, electric atmosphere

### Sunday — Recovery & Departure
- **Central Market Hall** for late breakfast / souvenir run
- **Gerbeaud Café** on Vörösmarty Square for coffee and cake
- Afternoon flights usually doable for a Sunday departure
